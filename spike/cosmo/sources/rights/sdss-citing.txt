Technical Publications to cite
We also ask that these papers cite the key technical publications of SDSS-V relevant to the data or software being used. This list will be updated over time.
The Full List of Technical Papers
All technical publications are automatically updated and can be found
here
.
Overview
All SDSS-V papers should cite the SDSS-V Overview Paper
Kollmeier et al. (2026)
.
MWM / BHM
All Milky Way Mapper and Black Hole Mapper papers should cite:
Gunn et al. (2006),
AJ, 131, 2332
(the SDSS Telescope)
Bowen & Vaughan (1973),
ApOpt, 12, 1430
(the Irénée du Pont Telescope)
Smee et al. (2013),
AJ, 126, 32
(the SDSS/BOSS spectrographs)
Wilson et al. (2019),
PASP, 131, 055001
(the APOGEE spectrographs)
LVM
All Local Volume Mapper papers should cite:
Drory et al. (2024),
AJ, 168, 198
(overview)
Blanc et al. (2024),
SPIE, 1309403
(instrument)
Herbst et al. (2024),
AJ, 168, 267
(telescope)
Sánchez et al. (2025),
AJ, 169, 52
(data analysis pipeline)
Perruchot, S., et al. (2018),
SPIE, 10702, 7
(DESI Spectrographs)
For a full list of current LVM technical papers, see the DR20
LVM Technical Papers
page.
