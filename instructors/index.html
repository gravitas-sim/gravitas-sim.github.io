Instructor Resources

Teaching guides, learning objectives, answer keys and classroom materials for the Gravitas investigations.

One shared passphrase, distributed to instructors. Contact the project if you need it for your course.

How this is protected

Gravitas is a static site with no server, so there is nowhere to check a password. Instead the materials are encrypted. The file your browser downloads is ciphertext; the passphrase you type derives the key that decrypts it, here in the browser. A wrong passphrase does not fail a check that could be bypassed, it simply cannot produce the key.

The honest limit: the encrypted file is publicly downloadable, so its security is the strength of the passphrase against an offline attack. The key derivation is deliberately slow to make guessing expensive. Please do not post the passphrase where it can be indexed.

Teaching with Gravitas and How Gravitas Models the Universe are public and need no passphrase. Share them with students and colleagues freely.

Collecting the work needs no passphrase either. Drop the lab reports your students hand in on the submission review page and it shows which questions the class found hardest. It reads the PDF, a progress file or a pasted submission token, keeps nothing, and sends nothing anywhere. It is not a gradebook: there is no roster, and it checks answers, not identity.

If you are evaluating a section rather than just teaching it, the classroom evidence kit has printable forms: an implementation checklist, a twelve-item pilot pre/post assessment, a usability questionnaire, an anonymous participant-code workflow, and de-identified CSV and JSON templates. They are project-developed and unvalidated, they collect no names or email addresses, and nothing they produce is sent anywhere.

Instructor Resources

Everything needed to assign Gravitas investigations in an introductory astronomy course.

General resources

Classroom activities

Shorter than an investigation and built from its steps: a five-minute demonstration, a twenty-minute guided pair task, a full lab. Each format has its own instructor guide, and most have a student worksheet. These are in the ZIP as well, but they are downloadable one at a time here because an instructor planning one period does not want the other fifty-three documents.

Investigation materials

Each investigation has an instructor guide and a separate answer key. Answer keys are generated from the live investigation definitions and checked against the same rule the website uses to mark student work, so they cannot disagree with what students see.
