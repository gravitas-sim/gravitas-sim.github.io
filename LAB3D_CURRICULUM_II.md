# The 3-D lab curriculum II (Prompt 271)

The 3-D lab (`/3d/`) gains instruments and five guides on what it was built to
teach: orbital elements as a language, changing reference planes, an orbit seen
from a chosen direction, mutual inclinations and a resonant pair. No renderer
was added. Each guide step names the run mode its task needs, and the lab
enforces it.

## 1. Run modes

| Mode | What it is | What it cannot show |
|---|---|---|
| `kepler` | every body on its own two-body orbit about its primary, advanced by the mean motion (`js/lab3d/runModes.js`) | libration, precession, secular exchange, an encounter: the elements never change |
| `nbody` | the validated kernel's integrator (`js/lab3d/live.js`), the scheme and step named by the system | (the reference mode) |
| `precomputed` | a recorded run played back with its provenance (`createReplaySession`) | anything past the recording; it is an in-memory run, no file format is defined |

The "Run mode" control and its indicator name the mode, the integrator and the
step (`describeMode`). `modeProblem(guide, step, labMode)` refuses a step that is
not in its declared mode (`needsNbody`, `needsKepler`, `needsPrecomputed`), and a
step with no declared mode (`undeclared`); a reading step runs anywhere. The
guide runner's "Do it for me" switches the mode; a reader switches it by hand.
`tests/lab3dCurriculumII.test.js` holds that an N-body step cannot run in
Kepler-only mode and that a libration is never asked of a Kepler-only step.

## 2. Instruments

- Orbit editor and sky view (`js/lab3d/view/orbitPanel.js`, lazy; words in
  `js/i18n/*.lab3dOrbit.js`): the six elements of a body about its primary, in the
  chosen plane, with the frame, epoch, time scale and units named through the
  orbital layer (148); Apply rebuilds only that body and what orbits it, every
  other body bit for bit (`editElements`). An uncertainty column appears when a
  value carries one; no guide system has fitted values, so none shows one here.
- Reference plane: the system's own axes, equatorial (solar units only; the J2000
  obliquity of the sky kernel), the invariable plane (total angular momentum about
  the barycenter) and an observer's sky (`planeAxes`). A plane is a choice of axes
  for the numbers shown; nothing is integrated in it.
- The sky: apparent ellipse, transit impact parameter and chord (`apparentEllipse`,
  `transitOf`), and the mutual inclination of a pair.
- A resonant pair: the resonant argument and the range it has seen.

## 3. Guides

| Guide | Mode | Steps in another mode | Depths |
|---|---|---|---|
| `l3-elements` The language of orbits | Kepler-only | none | core, advanced |
| `l3-frames` Changing frames | Kepler-only | none; the node's precession is stated, not shown | core, advanced |
| `l3-seen` Seen from here | Kepler-only | none | core, advanced |
| `l3-tilts` Mutual inclinations | Kepler-only for the geometry | the R6 Kozai-Lidov steps are N-body | core, advanced |
| `l3-resonant` Resonant pairs | N-body, a window of 600 inner orbits | the three steps that show the absence of libration are Kepler-only | core, advanced |

The window of 600 orbits is 24,000 ticks (40 an orbit) and holds 5.3 libration
periods of the measured 112 orbits. The answer key is `js/data/lab3dAnswerKey.js`,
regenerated by `tools/lab3d-guides-key.mjs --write`.

## 4. Validation

`tests/fixtures/lab3d-curriculum-ii/thresholds.json` was committed before any
instrument (38 legs; amendment AM1 adds H1; AM2 adds E2s, E4s, E7s).
`oracle.py` is independent (`python3 -E`, numpy 1.26.4 and scipy 1.13.1, no
repository code). `tools/lab3d-curriculum-ii-validate.mjs` builds its inputs,
judges the kernel side and writes `results.json`; the test runs it again.

- 34 legs are measured here and pass, among them the editor's round trip (A1, A3,
  A4), the typed path equal to the kernel bit for bit (A5), the planes against the
  oracle to 1.4e-14 rad (B1), the apparent ellipse to 3e-8 (C1), the transit to
  3e-10 star radii (C3), Kepler-only positions to 4e-14 of a (D1), the replay
  bit for bit (D4), the resonant argument to 0.009 rad over the window (E1), the
  node precession of the frames guide, -1.265 degrees per 100 orbits (H1), and the
  Kozai-Lidov peak (F3).
- Two legs fail as committed and stay failing, labelled: E2 and E7. Sampled once per
  inner orbit the argument carries a short-period term, so the maxima are that term's
  (15.3 against 17.5 orbits, kernel against oracle); that is a defect of the
  measurement. AM2 was recorded before the smoothed legs ran: E2s, E4s and E7s pass
  (kernel 112.38, oracle 112.41 orbits; the guide states 112 orbits and 0.55 rad).
- Four first runs were not clean and are kept in `results.json` under `history`: B4
  (sky axes lost digits near a pole, fixed in `planeAxes`), C1 and C3 (the oracle was
  below its committed resolution), F3 (the validator did not continue a full session).
- Not measured by the validator: G1 (the key, `tests/lab3dGuides.test.js`), G3 (axe and
  keyboard, `e2e/`), G4 (route ceilings, `tools/route-budgets.json`; raised by their measured values under the standing approval, rows D-BUDGET-271-01 to 03), G5 (the closure).

## 5. Limits and what is held

- Spanish is unreviewed by a fluent reader; it is index-free (keyed) and appended.
- The roadmap's link from "Seen from here" to the transit and direct-imaging work
  (225, 230) is not made: those investigations have no id to link to yet.
- The element editor has no fit to draw uncertainties from in these systems.
- A precomputed run is an in-memory replay; opening one from a file is not defined.
- No closure file was added to the instructor bundle's source closure.
