## Suggested attribution

For a course pack, a slide, or a derived worksheet:

> Adapted from *Gravitas* by Carl Ziegler, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
> https://gravitas-sim.online

For a translation or a substantially modified investigation, say so:

> Based on the investigation "Kepler's Laws" from *Gravitas* by Carl Ziegler
> (CC BY 4.0), translated into Portuguese and shortened.

If you are citing Gravitas in a paper rather than reusing its material, use
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) instead — that is a citation, not an attribution,
and the two are different requests.
