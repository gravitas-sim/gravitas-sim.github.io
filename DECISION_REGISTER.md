# Decision register

The single list of decisions later work may rely on. It has one row per decision, taken from:
- the gate documents and RFCs of the first roadmap;
- the decision sections of the suite documents;
- the binding decisions both roadmaps carry forward.

A verdict a document states but this register does not list is evidence, not permission.

**The count at creation (2026-09-29):** 82 rows.
- **RATIFIED:** 78.
  - 4 reviewed by Carl;
  - 74 ratified by delegation under his standing instruction of 2026-09-29, which is not a review of the verdict.
- **PROPOSED:** 4.
- **DELEGATED, SUPERSEDED, REVERSED:** none.

Every row ratified by delegation is on Carl's ratification docket, which sits outside the repository beside the roadmaps. Each stays his to review, revise or reverse.

## How to use this register

- **Statuses.** A row has one of five:
  - **RATIFIED**: Carl has confirmed it, directly or by the delegation his standing instruction of 2026-09-29 gives. The row says which, when and where.
  - **DELEGATED**: decided on a recommendation while Carl was away, and not yet ratified in either way.
  - **SUPERSEDED**: replaced by a later decision, which the row names.
  - **REVERSED**: withdrawn. The row says by whom, when and why.
  - **PROPOSED**: a decision Roadmap II introduces, adopted when the prompts it names land.
- **Building on a row:**
  - a prompt may build on a **RATIFIED** row;
  - it may build on a **DELEGATED** row only if its Run condition says so, and it records the dependency in its PR;
  - it may never build on a **REVERSED** row.
- **Changing a status:** Carl edits the row, in a one-line commit that says why. A delegated ratification he reviews becomes "Reviewed by Carl, <date>" in the fifth column; a verdict he rejects becomes **REVERSED**, with the reason.
- **Adding decisions:** a new gate adds its rows when its decision document lands. tests/decisionRegister.test.js fails if any `*_GATE.md` or `*_RFC.md` has no row.
- **The Roadmap II prompts column** names the prompts that rely on the row, as the roadmap's run conditions do.
